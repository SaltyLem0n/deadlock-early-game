# UI sources and attribution

This is an independent ICT324 research showcase. It is not the Deadlock Wiki and is not affiliated with Valve.

The visual shell, brown theme, four numbered stylesheets, background, font declarations, navigation imagery and local asset collection are adapted from the public [Deadlock Wiki](https://deadlock.wiki/) homepage extraction captured on 27 October 2026. The adaptation replaces wiki-specific content and backend controls with project content and adds responsive layouts, accessible controls and project interactions.

`ui-asset-manifest.json` preserves the extraction's source URLs, asset filenames, byte counts, SHA-256 hashes and captured revision. Its modification notes describe the extraction, rather than this research-site adaptation. The four numbered stylesheets and 225 captured assets are retained byte-for-byte. Project changes live in `index.html`, `app.js` and `styles/research.css`.

Fonts retain their original ownership and source notices, including the extracted Valve Occult, Retail Demo and Inter files. The research proposal and project research content are separate from the source wiki's text.
